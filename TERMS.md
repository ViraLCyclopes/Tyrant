- Tyrant changes your game folder when you ask it to (it installs MelonLoader, Tyrant's dumper and framework, and mods).
  It records what it adds so it can remove it, but keep a backup of anything you cannot lose, such as save games.
- Game updates can break mods and the framework until they are updated.
- You use Tyrant at your own risk. Nobody involved in making it is liable for lost data, broken saves or damage to your
  game, as far as the law allows.
